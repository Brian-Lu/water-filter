Basement layout, drawn to scale

Space: 40" wide × 36" deep × 80" high (concrete floor to the overhead pipe run), unfinished area along the main. Equipment sizes are from the manufacturers' spec sheets. Water flows left to right: meter → sediment → LR-BB50 → FloPlus → softener → UV → house.

1. Front view: Pentair LR-BB50 + FloPlus Protect row

Two Pentair 20" Big Blue housings, LR-BB50 (lead/cyst, 27.2" × 7") and FloPlus Protect 20BB (PFAS incl. PFNA, 23.2" × 7.45"), next to the sediment housing. Cartridges come out by unscrewing the bowl downward, so the overhead pipe cap is irrelevant. Sizes from Pentair spec sheets; housings on a shared bracket with close nipples (tight-width arrangement, plumber should aim for it).

Width check: 7.5 + 7 + 7.45 ≈ 22" of housings + two 1.5" nipples ≈ 25", a ~4" gap, then the 9" resin tank ≈ 38" of the 40"; the inlet valve/union probably has to drop in from above rather than come in from the side. Tight: plumber must confirm on site; moving the sediment housing upstream on the main frees ~9". Depth is not an issue (27" needed of 36").

2. Top view (looking down)

The brine tank is the widest item (18"). Putting it in front of the resin tank instead of beside it is what makes 40" work; the two are joined by a small 3/8" brine line, so the salt tank can sit anywhere within a few feet. Total depth from the wall is ~26" (resin tank 9" + gap + brine tank 15"), well inside the 36" available.

For the record: the Enpress PIONEER tank that was considered and dropped (needs ~86" of ceiling to pull its 40" cartridge; the spot has 80")

A. PIONEER row, front view

Widths add up: housing 7.5" + PIONEER 8" + resin tank 9" = 24.5" of equipment, with 5–7" between units for unions and the bypass loops ≈ 36–40" of the 40". Tight: the plumber should confirm the valve/union layout on site. The brine tank is drawn translucent because it stands in front of the resin tank, not beside it (36" of depth is plenty). Pipe routing is schematic; the plumber will pick the real path.

B. The cartridge-change problem

Same scale in all three: green = PIONEER tank (~50"), orange = the 40" Pb cartridge being pulled, grey = the overhead pipes 80" above the floor. Enpress wants 36" above the tank; there is ~30".

Enpress asks for 3 ft above the tank; we have ~30". B is the clean fix if any 8" gap in the overhead pipes lines up (the tank just shifts left/right). C is the fallback: because the E3-M's inlet, outlet and bypass are at the base (Enpress schematic) and the tank is light plastic, undoing the two unions lets it lean. C is common field practice, not an Enpress-published procedure; the plumber should confirm he's fine with it. Either way it's a once-a-year job.
