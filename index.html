Where it comes from, and when to order

  • No single supplier carries everything. ESP Water Products (UV — order first, 4–6 weeks), Fresh Water Systems (all four Pentair/Pentek housings and their cartridges), SupplyHouse (softener), Home Depot / Brondell / Waterdrop (RO, fallback softener).
  • No water-treatment dealer takes back equipment once water has been through it (defects go through warranty), so: boxes stay sealed until the plumber is on site, the row is dry-fitted before anything is wetted, and the softener is bought after the dry-fit confirms the width. Fresh Water Systems and ESP allow 30 days unused; SupplyHouse 90 days; Home Depot 90 days in store.
  • Plumber: a labour quote and an install date lets the homeowner time the Fresh Water Systems order to land within 3 weeks of the install.

3. The spot

  • Unfinished basement, concrete floor: 65" wide × 36" deep × 80" high (floor to overhead pipe run) once the laundry sink is moved out. Homeowner measurement, please confirm.
  • Row, right to left: inlet drop (V1, gauge, sample cock) 5.5" → sediment 7.5" → 1.5" close nipple → LR-BB50 7" → tee T1 5" → FloPlus A 7.5" → 6" (jumper elbows) → FloPlus B 7.5" → tee T2 5" → softener resin tank 10.2" ≈ 62.7" of the 65". Brine tank in front of the resin tank (≈31–34" of the 36" depth). UV horizontal above the housings. Height is never the constraint (top pipe ≈ 76").
  • It only closes with close nipples between the housings and the valves at the row ends (V1/V2/V3); a union and a valve at every housing does not fit. If the dry-fitted row leaves under ~12" for the resin tank, use the Rheem RHS42 cabinet softener (15.25" wide, fits with ~6" to spare because its brine is inside) or move the sediment housing upstream on the main (frees ~9").
  • Without a softener the row is ~50", with 13"+ spare.
  • Run the row in 1" (copper or PEX) even if the house line is 3/4"; full-port ball valves at the row ends, unions on every unit (U1–U8), one bypass loop around the whole row.
  • Cartridges change from below: bowls hang at 29–33" off the floor, so no step and no tipping anything. UV lamp pulls sideways into open air. Salt goes in from the front.
  • Every valve, union, gauge, drain and the heights/centrelines are on piping.html; the full to-scale drawings and the fallback are on layout.html.

4. Confirm on the site visit

5. Install-day asks

  • Dry-fit first: hang all four housings and set the resin tank in place before any water goes in, so anything wrong can still go back to the vendor.
  • Valves at the row ends + unions on every device (tags V1–V3, U1–U8 on piping.html), bypass loop around the row, softener on its own bypass too. The two FloPlus jumpers the same length, no valves on the branches.
  • Pressure test and leak check before leaving; flush carbon cartridges to drain per Pentair (fines).
  • Leak sensor with auto-shutoff valve on the main.
  • Label each valve (bypass, in, out) and the drain line.
  • Show the homeowner one cartridge change (bowl down, O-ring grease, burp air).
  • Softener programmed from a measured hardness test on the day (~3–4 grains expected); calendar override ≤ 14 days so the standpipe trap stays wet.

Putting the row together (cross-unit rules from the manuals)
  1. Order is fixed: sediment → LR-BB50 → FloPlus A ∥ FloPlus B → softener → UV. Viqua: a 5-µm sediment filter must precede the UV and the UV "should be the final piece of treatment equipment"; the softener goes after the carbon stage (chlorine shortens resin life) and before the water heater, not on a short run to it (Pentair manual §7).
  2. Shut-off valve before the row (Pentair requires one before each filter); valves at the row ends and unions on both sides of every unit; one bypass around the whole row; the softener's included bypass stays on. No valves on the two FloPlus branches.
  3. Run the row in 1". All four Pentair caps are 1" NPT; the Pentair softener ships both 1" and 3/4" MNPT connectors — use the 1".
  4. Pressure: everything is happy at 30–80 psi (Pentair housings 30–90, Pentair softener 20–125 with 60 optimum; PRV if daytime pressure is over 80). Cold water only, 40–100°F, no freezing, no direct sun.
  5. Grounding: if the copper main is the electrical ground, a #8 AWG (or larger) jumper around the plastic housings, clamped both ends per NEC (Pentair LR-BB50 manual, p.4).
  6. Support the housings on their brackets so the pipe carries no weight (Pentair); bracket top ≥34" off the floor so there is 5" under each bowl for cartridge changes (FloPlus manual, p.4).
  7. Thread sealant: tape on pipe-fitting threads is fine everywhere. Never point a torch at the softener valve (thermoplastic) and never sweat pipe with water in it (steam damages the valve); don't solder with the Viqua attached (O-rings).
  8. Electrical: two 120 V receptacles at the row, the UV one GFCI (Viqua: grounded, no adapters or extension cords); the softener's 12 V transformer within cord reach of the valve. Third outlet under the kitchen sink only if the Waterdrop RO is chosen (the Brondell has no power).
  9. Drain: softener drain 1/2" ID tubing (not supplied — the valve takes a standard 3/4" NPT drain fitting) to a 2" trapped standpipe built on the old laundry-sink drain, ≥1.5" air gap, run ≤ 25 ft, no dips. Pentair's manual lists floor drain, utility sink, standpipe or stack as acceptable, always with an air gap.
  10. Start-up order: fill and flush each Pentair housing (15 min LR-BB50, 5 min each FloPlus at 5 gpm) → softener: fill the tank slowly through the bypass, program time/day/hardness/regen time, run a manual regeneration to the drain and add salt → sanitize the whole system with household bleach per Viqua §3.2, then light the UV → RO under the sink last.
  11. Pressure-test and leak-check, then show the homeowner one Big Blue cartridge change (steps below).
Sediment housing: Pentek 150233 + Neo-Pure MB-45200-05 cartridge
  • Spec sheet: polypropylene 20" Big Blue housing, HFPP cap with 1" NPT in/out and a pressure-relief button on the inlet side; 23.22" × 7.45"; 40–100°F; max 90 psi; Buna-N O-ring; NSF/ANSI 42 (structural). Takes any 4.5" × 20" cartridge.
  • Pentair publishes no stand-alone install sheet for the bare housing. Install it exactly like the FloPlus (same housing, same bracket pattern): tape threads, adapters hand-started then wrench-tight, "IN" toward the meter, two wrenches on the unions, bracket top ≥34" off the floor.
  • FWS stainless single bracket (or Pentek 150061); the housing wrench supplied with the two Pentair kits fits it, so one wrench serves all three.
  • Cartridge Neo-Pure MB-45200-05 (graded-density polypropylene, 50→5 µm, 4.5" × 20", NSF/ANSI 42 material/structural, fits Pentek Big Blue): change every 3–6 months or when PG1 − PG2 climbs noticeably. Same change procedure as the LR-BB50 below. Pentek DGD-5005-20 is the interchangeable Pentair-branded equivalent.
  • Housing bottom (bowl) should be replaced every 10 years (opaque); date it at install (Pentair caution, both manuals).
Lead / cyst: Pentair LR-BB50 (cartridge LR-FB20BB, #655210-43)

Specs (manual p.4, spec sheet): 1" NPT molded into the head (3/4" reducer bushings included); 10 gpm; 30–90 psi; 40–100°F; cold water only; rated capacity 35,000 gal on the spec sheet (the manual's data sheet says 40,000; plan on 35,000). Kit includes cartridge, wrench, mounting bracket and screws. NSF/ANSI 53 lead (pH 6.5 and 8.5) and cyst, CSA B483.1.

Install (manual pp. 4–5):

  1. Shut-off valve before the filter; install after the water meter. Allow at least 1-1/2" under the housing (manual minimum; we want 5" like the FloPlus).
  2. About three wraps of plumber's tape, clockwise, on each fitting; start adapters into the cap by hand, then wrench firm, do not over-tighten.
  3. Measure across the assembled fittings and subtract 1-1/2" for 1" pipe (1" for 3/4" pipe); mark the pipe to remove.
  4. Shut the main, open the lowest faucet to drain, cut the pipe, file the ends square; bucket under.
  5. Set the assembly with the cap's "IN" facing the incoming supply; spread the pipe ends; two adjustable wrenches, one holding the adapter, one on the nut; repeat on the outlet.
  6. Open the water slowly, press the red relief button to burp air, check for leaks, open the nearest faucet and flush 15 minutes.
  7. Jumper wire (#8 AWG min.) around the filter if the pipes ground the house; support the filter's weight so it doesn't stress the pipe.

Cartridge change (manual pp. 6–7): turn the cap's bypass handle clockwise, press the red relief button, bucket under, unscrew the bowl with the wrench, lift out the big O-ring and wipe it, discard the cartridge, rinse the bowl, lubricate the O-ring with clean silicone grease and seat it level in its groove, drop in the new cartridge over the standpipe, hand-tighten the bowl (snug, not wrenched), open water slowly, press the relief button, flush 15 minutes, check for drips. A drip at the bowl = reseat the O-ring; a drip at a fitting = shut off, relieve pressure, tighten about 1/2 turn. Pentair customer service 800-279-9404.

PFAS / chlorine: Pentair FloPlus Protect 20BB (cartridge #655230-43)

Specs (manual p.3): 30–90 psi; 5 gpm; 40–100°F; 40,000 gal. System includes housing, cartridge, mounting bracket and housing wrench. Tools: pencil, two adjustable wrenches, file, screwdriver, pipe cutter or hacksaw. Galvanized fittings must be used on galvanized pipe. Cold water only; not in direct sunlight.

Install (manual p.4):

  1. Load the cartridge into the housing first (per the replacement steps), then install after the meter.
  2. Shut the cold supply and open the nearest faucet to relieve pressure.
  3. Assemble fittings into the cap by hand, then wrench-firm (no over-tightening). Measure across the fittings, subtract 1-1/2" for 1" pipe, mark and cut the main, thread/tape the ends.
  4. Cap "IN" toward the incoming supply; two wrenches on the inlet adapter, then the outlet.
  5. Jumper wire if the pipes ground the electrical system.
  6. Screw the bracket to the cap, bracket to the wall, top of bracket no less than 34" off the floor so there is 5" below the housing.
  7. Fill slowly, press the red relief button, flush 5 minutes at 5 gpm before use, check for leaks before leaving.

Cartridge change (manual pp. 4–5): shut off, press the relief button, bucket, bowl off (turn clockwise looking up), remove and wipe the O-ring, discard the cartridge, rinse the bowl, 1/3 water + 2 tablespoons bleach, scrub with a non-abrasive sponge, rinse; silicone-grease the O-ring and seat it level; new cartridge over the standpipe; bowl hand-tight; water on slowly; relief button; flush 5 minutes at 5 gpm. If shutting the filter off also kills the house water, fill a bucket first. Bowl replaced every 10 years.

Softener: Pentair WaterTrust Pro 35974

Specs (spec sheet, manual p.4): 1.0 cu ft resin, 33,500 grains, 11 gpm service flow, 2.7 gpm to drain, 20–125 psi (60 optimum), 40–100°F, 120 V in / 12 V out transformer, resin tank 10.2" × 51.6" with the Autotrol valve on top, round brine tank 18.5" × 33.5" (250 lb salt). Systems tested and certified by WQA to NSF/ANSI 44 & 372 and CSA B483.1. 10-year limited warranty.

Site (manual §4): level smooth floor; as close as practical to the main, a drain (floor drain, sewer trap, utility sink, standpipe or stack per local code) and an outlet; drain run under 25 ft; bypass outside faucets. Do not lay the unit on its side in transport; use a hand truck.

Plumbing (§5–8): shut off at the meter and drain the pipes — never sweat pipe with water in it (steam damages the valve) and never point the torch at the unit. Connect the house line to the bypass valve: seen from the front, the inlet is the left port and the outlet the right (so with right-to-left flow the lines cross behind the valve — drawn that way on layout.html). 1" and 3/4" MNPT connectors are supplied; avoid a short run to the water heater (hot water must not back into the softener; check valve only as a last resort). Connect the 3/8" brine line to the brine tank. If the copper main is the electrical ground, bond around the plastic valve (§8).

Drain (§7): 1/2" ID flexible tubing (not supplied) on the valve's 3/4" NPT drain fitting, to an approved drain with an air gap; may be rigid pipe or PEX if code requires. Brine-tank overflow fitting is gravity only.

Start-up and programming (pp. 9–13): with the bypass in bypass, open the inlet slowly to fill the tank and purge air, then open fully; set time of day, day of week, hardness (grains per gallon from the day's test), regeneration time (default 2:00 AM) and salt dosage in Level I; set a calendar-day override (≤ 14 days here) so the trap stays wet; run a manual regeneration (REGEN) and check the drain flows; fill the brine tank with solar or block salt (manual's two recommended kinds). Test hardness twice a year with strips.

UV: Viqua VH410-V

Requirements (manual §1 safety, §3.1): plug into a grounded receptacle protected by a GFCI; no adapters, no extension cords; indoor, cold line only, before any branch lines, last treatment step; 5-µm sediment filter upstream; shut off water and release pressure before any service. Metal fittings and at least 10" of copper pipe on the outlet (heat and UV degrade plastic). Lamp contains mercury: never vacuum a broken lamp.

Mounting (§3.1): vertical with the inlet at the bottom and the lamp connector on top is preferred; horizontal is allowed only with the outlet port pointing up so air purges. Leave clearance equal to the chamber's own length at the lamp end to pull the lamp and quartz sleeve. Controller mounted above or beside the chamber, horizontally, with drip loops in every cord. Wall clamps are supplied; union connectors recommended; do not solder with the chamber connected (O-rings). Optional: flow restrictor on the outlet (direction-arrowed), bypass assembly, solenoid valve.

Start-up (§3.2): the whole downstream system, including the water heater, must be sanitized by flushing with household bleach before the UV is put in service. Inspect for water on the controller or lamp connector before plugging in.

Service (§4): lamp rated ~9,000 hours → replace annually with a genuine Viqua lamp (S410RL-HO), then reset the lamp timer (§5.1.3); quartz sleeve cleaned with CLR or vinegar when the UV-intensity reading drops, replaced if it won't come clean; clean the UV sensor at the same time (§4.3). Lamp change is a no-tools job; see the Viqua videos linked above.

Kitchen RO option A: Brondell Capella RC250 (tank type, air-gap faucet)

Specs (manual, product specifications): three-stage: Activated Carbon Plus (6 months), E2RO membrane (24 months), carbon block (6 months); filter hanger 13.5" L × 3.75" W × 13.5" H; 3.2-gal storage tank with stand 8.5" dia × 14.5" H, pre-charged to 6 psi; 35–100 psi feed, 39–100°F; 0.5 gpm service flow; WQA-certified to NSF/ANSI 42, 53, 58 and 401 (lead, cyst, PFOA/PFOS, chlorine, TDS and more per the performance data sheet). No power. One-year warranty.

Install (manual, steps 1–6): water-supply T-valve on the 3/8" cold line under the sink (adapters supplied); air-gap faucet in an existing hole of at least 1" (or a new hole — plumber); drain saddle on the tailpiece above the trap; tank ball valve on the tank (Teflon tape), tank stood in the cabinet; 1/4" colour-coded tubing: feed → unit, unit → tank, unit → faucet, faucet air-gap drain stub → drain saddle (brine/waste runs up to the faucet base and back down — this is what satisfies PCNYS 611.2). Flush per the manual before use.

Upkeep: RF-30 pre/post set every 6 months (~$80), RF-50 membrane every 24 months (~$80); twist-and-seal cartridges; LED on the faucet reminds when due.

Kitchen RO option B: Waterdrop G3P800 (tankless, 800 GPD)

Install (manual pp. 6–11, steps 1–8):

  1. Feed-water adapter on the cold line under the sink (cold only).
  2. RO faucet in its own hole (1-1/4"), with the small UV sterilizer pushed onto the faucet stem (output end, the one without the lamp ring) and locked with the clip.
  3. Drain saddle on the sink drain pipe with the foam seal aligned to the hole; pick a spot that's easy to reach.
  4. Stand the housing in the cabinet: 18.12" × 5.67" × 17.72", so ~19" clear height.
  5. Tubing (1/4" PE, push-fit, insert to the mark on the tube, lock clip on): feed adapter → INPUT; FILTERED → UV sterilizer → faucet; drain saddle → WASTE.
  6. Power: faucet cord → FAUCET socket, UV cord → UV STERILIZER socket (keyed, won't go in wrong), power adapter → POWER port, then the wall outlet under the sink.
  7. Install the three filters (CF, CB, RO) and start up / flush per step 8 before drinking.

Operation and upkeep (pp. 13–18): TDS readout on the faucet, expect 90%+ rejection after the initial flush; the UV ring lights only while the faucet runs; the unit self-flushes 20 s after every 2 hours of run time and after a power cut. Filter lives: CF up to 6 months / 800 gal, CB 12 months / 1,600 gal, RO membrane 24 months / 3,200 gal (life reminder on the display; swap guide p.15). Waterdrop support 1-888-352-3558.
